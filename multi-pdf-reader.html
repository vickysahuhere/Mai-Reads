Why use a multi-document study binder?

The Chaos of Traditional Document Reading

When studying for exams or writing research papers, having 15 different browser tabs open wastes working memory and drains laptop battery. Finding where you left off in each PDF takes minutes.

The Solution: Unified Study Binders

  • Single Workspace: Organize multiple PDF and Word documents into colored binders.
  • 1-Click Tab Strip: Instantly switch between lecture slides, textbooks, and problem sets.
  • Persistent State: Each file remembers its zoom, scroll position, and text highlights independently.
  • Built-in Scratchpad: Write notes directly alongside your reading material with local auto-save.
