FIELD MANUAL & SPECIFICATION

A Sanctuary for Unhurried Reading

Mai-Reads is engineered specifically for deep research, prolonged study, and distraction-free comprehension. Built with a local-first architecture, it turns your browser into a calm, private reading environment.

Instant Memory Precision Highlighter 60/120fps Zoom Paper Brightness Modes & Navigation Shortcuts Local-First Privacy Maithil Studios
01 / PERSISTENCE

Instant Memory: Remembers Where You Left Off

When you reopen Mai-Reads after closing your laptop or shutting down your browser, your reading session is restored down to the exact millimeter.

Fractional Viewport Tracking

Rather than simply remembering a page number, Mai-Reads computes your exact scroll ratio (scrollY / totalHeight). When re-rendered at any window size, your reading view aligns precisely where your eyes last rested.

Dual-Tier Offline Storage

Document binaries are held safely in browser IndexedDB, while view states, bookmarks, and highlight arrays reside in atomic localStorage. Nothing is ever sent to an external server.

1-Click Welcome Resume

Upon launching the app, the welcome card immediately surfaces your last-opened document with page count and progress. One tap launches you straight back into your text without browsing your filesystem.

Reading History Sidebar

Press H or click the History icon to inspect recent books and papers. Each entry retains its progress percentage, last-read timestamp, and persistent annotations.

02 / ANNOTATION ENGINE

The Precision Highlighter System

Highlighting in Mai-Reads works on both digital documents and scanned PDFs without selectable text layers, with full permanent PDF export.

  • 1

    Text Selection Pill

    Select any sentence or paragraph with your cursor or finger. An ergonomic golden tooltip immediately appears above your selection with Highlight and Copy actions. One click creates a luminous, paper-warm highlight band.

  • 2

    Freehand Draw Tool (Pen Mode)

    Click the Pen icon in the floating dock or press D to enter Draw Mode. Click and drag anywhere across the page to highlight diagrams, mathematical formulas, footnotes, code snippets, or scanned archives where text cannot be selected.

  • 3

    Resolution-Independent Percentage Anchors

    Highlights are mathematically mapped as relative percentages (x%, y%, w%, h%) of each page canvas. Whether you zoom to 60%, 150%, or 200%, or resize your browser window, your highlights stay pinned with absolute fidelity.

  • 4

    1-Click Quick Erase

    Made a mistake or finished a review? Simply click or tap any existing highlight band to erase it immediately.

  • 5

    Baked-In Client-Side PDF Export

    Click Save & Export in the reader controls. Mai-Reads uses pdf-lib in-browser to permanently bake vector highlight rectangles into the actual PDF binary. The downloaded file can be opened in Adobe Acrobat, Apple Preview, or Google Drive with all your highlights intact.

03 / PERFORMANCE

Hardware-Accelerated 60/120fps Smooth Zoom

Traditional web PDF readers lag, jitter, and drop frames when zooming because they force heavy synchronous canvas re-rasterization on every pixel of movement. Mai-Reads solves this with a modern dual-stage graphics pipeline.

Stage 1: GPU Visual Scaling

When dragging the slider or pressing + / −, scaling executes directly on your GPU via hardware-accelerated CSS transforms. Frame rates remain rock-solid at 60Hz or 120Hz ProMotion without stutter.

Stage 2: Debounced Rasterization

Once zoom interaction pauses for 250ms, a lightweight background worker rasterizes sharp, retina-grade 2x resolution canvases for visible pages, giving you crisp vector clarity without scroll interruption.

Anchor-Stabilized Viewport

During zoom adjustments, Mai-Reads locks onto the center-vertical coordinate of the viewport. This completely eliminates the disorienting "rubbery bounce" effect and prevents unexpected jumping.

Tactile Zoom Controls

Includes quick 10% step buttons (− / +), a 1-click reset to 100%, pinch-to-zoom gestures, and a fluid range slider extending up to 200%.

04 / OCULAR ERGONOMICS

Paper Brightness & Sensory Calm

Modern displays output intense white light that strains the eyes during prolonged research sessions. Mai-Reads introduces independent paper attenuation.

Independent Paper Dimming (55% to 100%)

Dim the document page itself without darkening your browser interface, operating system, or secondary monitors. This provides instant relief for readers experiencing asthenopia, photophobia, or migraines.

Cognitive Design for ADHD & Dyslexia

All menus and controls fade into the background while reading, leaving only pure text on paper. Zero flashing banners, zero popups, and generous line-spacing allow uninterrupted hyper-focus.

05 / MODES & FORMATS

Reading Modes & Document Formats

Customize your reading environment to suit the material at hand, from continuous papers to single-page slide decks.

Continuous vs. Single Page Mode

Toggle between smooth continuous vertical scrolling and single-page presentation snap (P). Ideal for research papers and presentation decks alike.

In-Document Full-Text Search

Press Ctrl/Cmd + F to search throughout the active document with instant jumping to match occurrences and real-time result counts.

Bookmarks & Nested TOC

Bookmark key pages with a single click and navigate multi-level PDF table of contents trees with seamless section jumping.

Native DOCX Rendering

Drop Microsoft Word .docx documents directly into Mai-Reads. The app uses mammoth.js client-side to convert Word styles into calm typographic pages.

07 / PRIVACY ARCHITECTURE

100% Local-First Privacy Guarantee

Mai-Reads was built on a fundamental principle: your books, contracts, academic papers, and private notes belong exclusively to you.

Zero Server Uploads

Documents are parsed, decoded, rendered, and highlighted entirely inside your browser sandbox. No bytes are sent over the network.

Complete Offline Availability

Powered by a standalone Service Worker, Mai-Reads runs seamlessly on flights, trains, or off-grid retreats with zero internet connection.
