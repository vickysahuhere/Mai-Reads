About Mai-Reads

Mai-Reads is a distraction-free document reader for PDF and DOCX files, designed for students, readers, and researchers. It opens selected files in the browser and keeps reading controls out of the way until they are needed.

Why Mai-Reads exists

The document stays at the center while reading controls remain hidden until requested. Mai-Reads is designed for people who want to open a document, settle into the page, and read without a busy interface competing for attention.

Frequently asked questions

What is Mai-Reads?

Mai-Reads is a minimal PDF and DOCX reader designed for distraction-free reading and study. It opens documents from your device in a clean, paper-like interface.

Can Mai-Reads open PDF and Word documents?

Yes. Mai-Reads supports PDF files and DOCX files. DOCX documents are converted into reflowable web content, so their original Word pagination may differ.

Do I need to upload my document?

No upload to a Mai-Reads document-storage service is required. You select a local file and the reader processes it in your browser. This local build does load its reading libraries from CDNs; it does not send the selected document to a Mai-Reads server.

Does Mai-Reads require an account?

No. The current reading experience has no account or sign-in flow.

Is Mai-Reads designed for studying?

Yes. Its minimal interface, page navigation, and reading layouts are designed to support focused reading and study.

Can I reduce the brightness of the document?

Yes. Paper brightness is controlled separately from the light or dark interface theme.

Can I read one page at a time or scroll continuously?

Yes. Choose One page for page-by-page scrolling or Continuous to move through the document vertically.

Does Mai-Reads remember where I stopped?

It stores reading position metadata in this browser. Select the same local file again to resume; the browser does not reopen it automatically.
