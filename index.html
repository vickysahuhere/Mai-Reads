About Mai-Reads (My Reads): Privacy-First Document Reader & Study Notebooks

Mai-Reads (also known as My Reads or MyReads) is a 100% local, lightning-fast, distraction-free document reader and multi-file study notebook workspace. We built it so students, researchers, and professionals can bundle, open, read, zoom, and highlight PDF and DOCX files securely in their browser with zero server uploads.

Why Choose Mai-Reads / My Reads for Document Study & Research

  • Multi-File Study Notebooks: Bundle related textbooks, lecture notes, syllabus guides, and research papers into unified binders with 1-click tab switching.
  • Integrated Study Scratchpad: Jot notes, chapter summaries, and citations directly beside your documents with local real-time auto-saving.
  • Offline capabilities: Instant resume via IndexedDB cache remembers your exact page, scroll ratio, and annotations for every document independently.
  • Zero Data Collection & Serverless: 100% private. Your documents, notebooks, and notes never leave your device.
  • Precision Text Highlighter: Select text or freehand draw highlight bands across scanned documents with client-side baked-in PDF export.
  • Silky 60/120fps Smooth Zoom: Mathematical anchor lock keeps your position stable without jitter or rubbery pulls.

Accessibility & Cognitive Ergonomics

Mai-Reads (My Reads) is intentionally designed to support readers with specific accessibility needs and medical conditions:

  • ADHD (Attention-Deficit/Hyperactivity Disorder): A completely distraction-free interface with hidden controls prevents attention loss and hyper-focus disruption.
  • Asthenopia (Digital Eye Strain) & Photophobia (Light Sensitivity): Independent paper brightness controls and a deep dark mode help readers with migraines, post-concussion syndrome, and severe eye strain.
  • Dyslexia & Processing Disorders: Clean, unhurried reading layouts (single-page or continuous) without ads or pop-ups reduce cognitive load.
  • Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD): The minimalist, calm UI prevents visual sensory overload.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Mai-Reads (My Reads)?

Mai-Reads (often searched as My Reads or MyReads) is a private, web-based PDF and DOCX reader and study notebook workspace designed for distraction-free reading, academic study, and research.

How do Study Notebooks work in Mai-Reads?

Study Notebooks allow you to group multiple documents into organized binders. You can switch between files with a 1-click top tab strip, take unified notes in the built-in scratchpad, and preserve progress for every document with 100% offline privacy.

Is Mai-Reads completely private and offline?

Yes, all processing and document caching happen locally using browser-native technologies (IndexedDB) with zero telemetry or cloud uploads.

Does it support Word Documents and PDFs?

Yes, both PDF and DOCX files are natively rendered directly inside your browser.
