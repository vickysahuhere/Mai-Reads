# Mai-Reads 📖

**Mai-Reads** is an ultra-minimalist, privacy-first document reader engineered for deep focus. Open a PDF or DOCX file from your device and read in a calm, distraction-free environment.

**Created by [Maithil Studios](https://maithilstudios.vercel.app/).**

## 🌟 Key Features

*   **100% Privacy & Local Processing:** Your documents never leave your device. All parsing, rendering, and highlighting are done natively in your browser using local CPU power. Zero servers, zero uploads.
*   **Extreme Minimalism ("God Mode" UI):** Designed to get out of your way. Borders, heavy text, and controls fade into the background. 
*   **Visual Marker & Local Export:** Draw highlights directly onto PDFs. Because processing is local, you can download a newly compiled PDF with your highlights permanently baked in, all without any backend server.
*   **Instant Resume Cache:** Mai-Reads automatically caches your most recently read documents locally. Simply click your document in the history sidebar to instantly jump back to where you left off.
*   **True Offline PWA Support:** Install Mai-Reads directly to your desktop or home screen. You can load the app and read heavy PDFs even when you have zero internet connection.
*   **Silky-Smooth Zoom:** Zooming via pinch-to-zoom or slider mathematically anchors your exact scroll position, preventing the document from jumping.
*   **Independent Brightness & Dark Mode:** Adjust document paper brightness entirely separately from the app's UI theme to reduce eye strain.

## 🚀 How to Use
No installation or account required.

1. Open https://mai-reads.vercel.app/ in your browser.
2. Select **Open your document** or drop a PDF/DOCX file anywhere on the screen.
3. Because it is a Progressive Web App, you can click the "Install" icon in your browser's URL bar to save it to your desktop for offline use.

## 🛠 Known Limitations
*   DOCX is converted to reflowable HTML, so its exact original Word pagination may differ.
*   Highlight exporting is currently supported for PDFs only.
